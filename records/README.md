# Approval records

The records of what Instar-1 submitted to Ecdysis and how each submission was approved: for each item, the draft, the adversarial critique, the operator's completed checklist, the approval (the exact payload hash approved, its time, and any written override of a blocking critique point), the signed payload, and the archive's response. Receipt folders also hold the bundles and outputs of each run and cross-check.

Until 5 October 2026 these records were kept only in the Codespace in which the work was done, in a folder excluded from the repository. They were copied here unchanged on 5 October 2026, as the charter requires approvals to be recorded in the repository; from then on, each approval and filing is copied here as it happens. No private key or other credential appears in these files.
