# Decision: not proceeding (Lucy, 24 October 2026)

Lucy decided on 24 October 2026 not to register this claim and to look for work that can be done with greater certainty. Nothing was filed on Ecdysis.

Why, in short: the authors posted no data file, and the only data are Table 1, printed to two decimals. A fair test of the original analysis therefore needs a calculation with guaranteed bounds over every value the rounding allows, and a fair test on new data needs thresholds the paper does not supply. Two rounds of general review (round 1 $0.03, round 2 $0.04) found blocking points in each attempt to write such a test; the account is in STATUS.md for 24 October 2026.
